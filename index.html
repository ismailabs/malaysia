ABOUT THE EXPERIMENT

Between
Places.

An independent exploration of Malaysian places and everyday rhythms, made with HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, and GSAP.

This is a creative development showcase, not a travel booking service or an official tourism campaign. The places and photographs are real; the journey is a small, personal editorial selection.

Designed to show how considered typography, motion, and interaction can give a place, a café, or a local business a distinct digital presence.
