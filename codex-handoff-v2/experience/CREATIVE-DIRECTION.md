# Creative direction: from page to lived environment

## Premise
A fictionalized **visual journey** across real Malaysia. The visitor begins with a geographically grounded abstract atlas and passes through real authentic images, procedural atmosphere, typography as landscape, and highly choreographed camera-like movement. Real facts, photographs and geographic labels stay factual; the dramatic *interface* may be imaginative.

**One-second test:** a visitor should not think 'another muted editorial site'. They should see an immediately legible, surprising geography-led encounter with Malaysia: deliberate graphic depth, confident typographic scale and a sense of motion/invitation. It must not require watching a long loader.

## Art direction hierarchy
1. **Geographic truth:** Mainland peninsula and Borneo spatially distinct, labels correct, Sabah in Malaysian Borneo, Langkawi in northwest Kedah, George Town on Penang island, Kuala Lumpur within Peninsular Malaysia. Do not distort geography to the point of false labeling. Source SVG/cartographic dataset with redistribution rights and credit.
2. **Cinematic staging:** changing color and lighting, broad negative/positive space, intentional focal point, aggressive transition thresholds, layer masks, animated path/strokes, silhouetted gradients, subtle parallax. Do not turn all pages into repetitive blur/opacity reveals.
3. **Documentary trust:** photograph actual places when claiming documentary imagery; no AI-generated documentary photographs. Surface bylines/credits on credit page.
4. **Artful persuasion:** studio finale feels authored and integral, with visible, keyboard-accessible outbound portfolio link.
5. **Functional underlayer:** readable captions, meaningful hierarchy and semantic content without GSAP. Motion must never be the sole way to understand destinations.

## Visual language (Codex may refine through prototypes)
- Dark, evocative opening with sharply illuminated map geography, not another pale hero grid.
- Destination palette phases responsive to location (urban night, heritage warm textures, bright island sea, highland forest/mist), all harmonized by a consistent typography and UI token system.
- Large spatial typography and clear, high-contrast small navigation; purposeful nonlinear compositions instead of an even panel grid.
- Imagery in distinct roles: authentic photographs are narrative evidence; procedural paths/glows/contours/gradients connect scenes and simulate camera depth.
- Responsive mobile redesign may use simplified composition while preserving the *same dramatic story beats*, not just hide all effects.

## Avoid
- Stock template vibes, copy-pasted card grids, generic glassmorphism everywhere, cursors blocking controls, low-contrast micro-labels, needless scroll hijacking, endless reveals, AI-created fake locations, effect quantity as proxy for craft, and 'Awwwards winner' or award claims.

## Visual review question per scene
What new state does the page reach after this interaction that would have been impossible in V1's quiet photo editorial? If answer is 'photo moves slightly and heading fades in', redesign it.
