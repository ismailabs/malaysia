## Licensing
Local images list original source URLs in `assets/images/sources.json`, and local fonts include OFL licenses. These records do not automatically clear newly sourced imagery. For every new asset, track photographer/author, source, URL, license, permissible use, attribution requirement, modification and verification date. Prohibit paid media and uncertain reuse.
