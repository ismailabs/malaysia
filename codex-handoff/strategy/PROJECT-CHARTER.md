## Hierarchy of outcomes
1. Correct, live and attributable Cloudflare production website, including functional CTA to portfolio.
2. Distinctive cinematic Malaysia art direction and compelling comprehensive narrative.
3. Crawlable, useful location pages backed by factual research and original writing.
4. Very advanced but intentional interactive motion; support reduced motion and mobile/touch.
5. Real browser/test evidence, accessibility and practical performance, clear rollback path.
6. Reliable privacy-respecting analytics, without inventing numbers or violating budget.

## Scope
IN: original experience, visual system, copywriting grounded in researched facts, creative motion, destination pages, semantic SEO, controlled asset sourcing, accessibility, click measurement, browser checks, Cloudflare deployment, Vercel redirect, Git traceability.
OUT: new payment products, backend/database, fabricated travel packages/pricing/reviews, paid API access, framework migration, unsolicited domain transfer, redesign of `ismailstudio.my`, guaranteeing ranking or conversion uplift, replacing user's paid accounts.

## Quantitative release gates
Defined in `quality/DEFINITION-OF-DONE.md`. Targets are to be measured, not assumed. Conversion ranking or sales impact require real traffic and cannot be guaranteed before launch. If a platform permission or prerequisite prevents full completion, record an actual blocker and preserve safe deliverables.
